# News

What each release says in the Discord server's #general channel: one short,
friendly post per version, in plain words for players. Each release PR
writes the new version's section here next to its CHANGELOG.md section, and
after the upload `tools/post_general.py` posts it, with the CurseForge link
added at the end. The changes are grouped by the player whose feedback
or report led to them, and each player is thanked once, by their Discord
name, after their group; changes nobody asked for go in a group of their
own with no thanks. This is the only report that names players. A version with no section here posts nothing in #general.
This file does not ship in the addon.

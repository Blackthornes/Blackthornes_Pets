# Notes for Claude

Read this before working in this repo. The owner runs one Claude project per
addon: this one (**Blackthornes_Pets**), **Blackthornes_Equipment**
(`Blackthornes/Blackthornes_Equipment`), **Blackthornes_Tradeskills**
(`Blackthornes/Blackthornes_Tradeskills`) and **Blackthornes_Farmer**
(`Blackthornes/Blackthornes_Farmer`). The projects cannot read each other's
memory, so what one needs to know about another lives in the repos, in this
file and in theirs. Keep them current: when a change here leans on something
new in another addon, or a standing rule from the owner applies to all of them,
add it to each file.

Nothing private goes in this file (no local paths, emails or account names).
It never ships: `.pkgmeta` leaves it out of the zip and
`tools/check_package.py` refuses it.

## What this addon is for

Everything around pets for **hunters and warlocks**: the hunter's pet and
stable, the warlock's demons. The first feature is the pet status panel in
the pets window and the hunter's feed warning (`Logic/PetStatus.lua`), built
on the `C_PetInfo` calls the probe found, and the food helper
(`Logic/Food.lua`, `Data/PetFood.lua`, foods by item ID with their
English name, IDs filled in by `tools/petfood_ids.py` from pfQuest, the
client's diet words mapped in `DIET_WORDS` so a German client works too):
food in the bags by diet, a secure
Feed button kept outside the window so the window still closes in combat,
a low food warning, and a small Feed button above the quest tracker while the
pet is hungry (`UI/FeedButton.lua`, placed by screen position rather than
anchored to the tracker, so the tracker can still move in combat), a "Pet
care" key binding (`Bindings.xml`, `UI/FeedKey.lua`: a hidden secure button
the key clicks, its macro kept up to date out of combat; since 2026-10-11 it
also calls the pet with none out, revives a dead one, `[@pet,dead]` and
`petDead` when its body is gone, and summons a warlock's last demon, kept per
character in `lastDemon`, never rename it; in combat with the pet alive it
casts Mend Pet, `[@pet,exists,nodead,combat]`; and a second key, "Dismiss pet",
is its own secure button `BlackthornesPetsDismissKey` that only casts Dismiss
Pet, 2026-10-11), and the
taming skills on a beast's tooltip (`Logic/Taming.lua`,
`UI/TamingTooltip.lua`), from `Data/BeastSkills.lua`, which is generated from
the vmangos world database's copy of Classic 1.12's CreatureSpellData, never
typed by hand. `Logic/TameRecord.lua` records, account wide, what a beast
teaches on Forever by reading the new pet's bar right after Tame Beast;
The owner's `/bpet tamed` output is how new Forever beasts get into the data.
The **Pet Compendium** (`Logic/Compendium.lua`, `UI/CompendiumWindow.lua`,
`/bpet compendium`, asked 2026-10-07) lists every tameable beast in three
layers, the later winning, since data from the Forever client beats anything
online: Classic (`Data/Compendium.lua`, generated by
`tools/make_compendium.py` from the same vmangos dump as BeastSkills plus
pfQuest's coordinates), then Forever (`Data/ForeverBeasts.lua`, generated by
`tools/forever_beasts.py`: SEEN from Farmer's `Data/Data_Harvested.lua` by
mob name, TAMED from the owner's Pets save (`tamedBeasts` only), FOUND typed
in the tool, each from a published source, kept in the project's private notes: no data site is named in the repo or the game), then this account's own tames.
Never hand-edit either data file. Tame records now also keep `family`,
`zone`, `x`, `y` and `speed` (added fields; nothing renamed). The Speed
column (a player asked, the owner agreed 2026-10-11) is Classic's
`base_attack_time` in seconds, since a Classic pet keeps the speed of the
beast it was tamed from; `TameRecord:PetSpeed` reads `UnitAttackSpeed("pet")`
out of combat at the tame (and once for an older tame when it is called
out, `NoteSpeed`), and that real speed wins. Sorting by Speed puts the
fastest first across every family (2026-10-11; it used to go family by
family, so turning it round barely moved anything with a box ticked).
Every heading follows the house rule: a click sorts, a second turns it
round, a third goes back to by beast (`CompendiumUI:SortBy`), whatever
is ticked. The list's scroll bar takes the mouse (`CompendiumUI.grip`,
wider than it looks), so holding it drags the list, never the window. The
family picker is a dropdown (`Widgets.CreateDropdown`, Tradeskills'
trimmed to one choice; a player asked, the owner agreed 2026-10-11),
sized to its longest family by `Widgets.DropdownWidth`. Before a release, fold
in the owner's tames and the latest Farmer harvest by rerunning
`forever_beasts.py`. The map pin is Equipment's `Logic/Waypoints.lua`.
Beside it: settings, a minimap
button and a read-only `/bpet probe` for the pet calls. The feature ideas are
in the project's brainstorm, not in the repo.

## How this repo was set up

It copies its sisters on purpose, most closely Equipment (the newest and
smallest), so check them before inventing a convention:

- **Layout** `Core/ Data/ Logic/ UI/ Probe/ tools/ docs/`, one module per
  file through `ns.NewModule`, no Ace3 or LibStub. `Core/Init.lua` is
  Equipment's (itself Farmer's), renamed. The global is `_G.BlackthornesPets`,
  the commands `/bpet` and `/pets`, the saved data `BlackthornesPetsDB`
  (account: window positions, minimap, debug) and `BlackthornesPetsCharDB`
  (per character, because pets belong to one character).
- **Palette** is the house one (`ns.COLORS`), with `good` and `bad` in
  Tradeskills' profit green and loss red. `UI/Theme.lua`, `UI/Widgets.lua`,
  `UI/Minimap.lua` and `UI/Settings.lua` are Equipment's with the gear parts
  taken out. The minimap button starts at 260 degrees, clear of Tradeskills'
  at 170, Farmer's at 200 and Equipment's at 230.
- **Tools** (`syntaxcheck`, `lintcalls`, `runtests`, `check_release`,
  `check_package` and their tests, `link.ps1`) are Equipment's with the names
  changed. If one of them gets a fix in a sister repo, bring it here too.
- **Tests** are `tools/test_*.lua`, found by name. `tools/testenv.lua` loads
  the files the TOC lists, so there is no second list to keep.
- **PRs** follow the sisters' habit: a "Before:" and an "After:" paragraph
  in plain words, then a short "How" naming the functions and tests touched,
  that the checks pass, and whether `/reload` is enough. Release PRs are
  `release/X.Y.Z` branches titled "Changelog for X.Y.Z".
- **API findings** go in `docs/FOREVER_API_FINDINGS.md`. Tradeskills' and
  Equipment's files of the same name record what the Forever client does for
  their areas; read them before relying on an API.

Nothing here calls the other addons, and nothing calls this one, apart
from the shared arrow table below and Blackthornes Butler's one read-only
call (see "What Butler calls here"). If that changes, write down the global and the calls
in both repos' `CLAUDE.md`, the way Farmer and Tradeskills do for prices
and goals.

## What Butler calls here

**Blackthornes_Butler** (`Blackthornes/Blackthornes_Butler`) tells the
player what to do with each bag item, and asks here whether the pet wants
it. It calls, inside a pcall and only when this addon is loaded:

```lua
_G.BlackthornesPets.Butler:Advise(itemID)  -- a table, or nil; never raises; reads only
```

`Logic/Butler.lua` answers for the character playing and the pet it has
out, nil when there is no hunter pet that eats (no pet, a warlock, a client
that names no diet), when the item is not in `Data/PetFood.lua`, or on any
error. Otherwise:

- `eats`: true when the pet eats the item (its diet, and not 30 or more
  levels below the pet); false for pet food of another diet or too low.
- `buff`: true for buff food (`Food:IsBuffFood`), which is fed last.
- `hours`: about how long all the food the pet is fed from the bags lasts,
  to a tenth of an hour, by the classic rule (35,000, 17,000 or 8,000
  happiness a piece by `Food:Effect`, 670 lost every 7.5 seconds); 0 with
  none, nil while the bags are not all read. The same for every item.
- `count`: how many of the item are carried among the food the pet is fed;
  0 for food it eats that is not carried; nil when not known (food it does
  not eat, buff food with "Never feed buff food" ticked, or bags not all
  read).

Butler asks once per bag item, a hundred in one frame, so the pet and the
food (`Food:Latest`, itself kept between bag events) are read once and kept
until `Food:BagsChanged` or the clock moves on (`Butler:Reading`); no timer.
Keep the global, `Butler` and `Advise(itemID)` with these fields stable; if
one has to change, change Butler's `Logic/Sisters.lua` too. Tested in
`tools/test_butler.lua`.

  addon as a German client.

  and a new fixed width must wrap or grow, never end in "...".

## The owner's standing rules (all the addons)

- **Every list is dark glass** (the owner, 2026-10-12: "keep this as the
  standard across all Blackthornes addons", after Farmer's Kills & Loot
  panel was given the look of Tradeskills' queue). Rows or lines take
  `glass` and `glassAlt` in turn, by place, with a 1 px `border` line
  (alpha 0.6) under each; under the mouse `glassHover` and a gold edge,
  only on that row; the row picked or open is `glassActive` with a bright
  gold edge down its left. Never the older `row`/`rowAlt` bands for a
  list, a panel's lines or cards. A gradient fades through
  `Theme.LightGradient` or is hidden, never with SetAlpha alone (it stayed
  lit on Forever). This rule is in every addon's notes; keep them the same.
- **"Always check that it's clear to post."** Anything that goes public (a
  release, a CurseForge page, a post) is checked for private or unexpected
  content first, and nothing is published or uploaded without the owner's
  go-ahead. He tags releases himself.
- **Keep his data safe.** Never commit saved variables, tokens or anything
  from his machine; the upload keys are repository secrets he sets himself,
  so never ask him to paste one anywhere.
- **Changelogs come from git history.** Work out what changed since the last
  tag from the merged PRs and commits (`git log vX.Y.Z..origin/main --merges`);
  never ask the owner what went in. Claude drafts the CHANGELOG section in a PR;
  The owner merges, pulls `main`, tags and pushes.
- **A checkout shows the real version number** (the owner,
  2026-10-12). `ns.RELEASED_VERSION` in `Core/Init.lua` is the newest
  `## X.Y.Z` in CHANGELOG.md, shown when the packager has not filled in the
  TOC's `@project-version@` (he plays from a clone). The release PR that
  writes the CHANGELOG section changes it too; `tools/check_release.py`
  refuses a tag it does not match, and `tools/test_check_release.py` fails
  while the two disagree. This rule is in every addon's notes.
- **Every reply that ships code ends with the GitHub Desktop steps,** naming
  the branch: merge the PR on github.com (Ready for review, then Merge pull
  request); in GitHub Desktop switch **Current branch** to **main** and click
  **Pull origin**; then say whether the game needs `/reload` or a full restart
  (a full restart when a file is added, removed or renamed, or the TOC
  changes). For a release, add: History, right-click the newest commit,
  Create Tag, `vX.Y.Z`, Push origin. Remind him to pull `main` first, or the
  tag lands on an old commit and nothing runs.
- **CurseForge descriptions are features only.** Leave out privacy notes and
  any explanation of public emotes or their switches. Those belong in the
  README and CHANGELOG.
- **Forever is not retail for classes either.** The owner: "keep in mind this is
  WoW: Forever." Hunters have classic-style talent trees, held on Forever in
  one trait tree per class (see Equipment's findings). Never assume a retail
  or classic pet API exists (happiness, loyalty, the stable, demon summons):
  check with `/bpet probe` and record what it finds in
  `docs/FOREVER_API_FINDINGS.md`.
- **Smooth play comes first, but never at the cost of looks** (the owner,
  2026-10-09, after a hitch while crafting). Nothing on a timer or
  OnUpdate rebuilds or re-places a whole list, map or set of markers when
  only one thing moved: keep what is drawn, and redo only rows or pins
  whose inputs changed (a per-row key, a version number). Bag, loot,
  cast, skill and profession-list events arrive in bursts, several per
  craft or loot: coalesce them, read only what they changed, and skip
  work for windows that are hidden. Work over hundreds of items (recipes,
  sources, saved spots) is spread over frames (a worker with a small
  per-frame budget) rather than done in one frame, and shown when done.
  Clicks and typing still answer at once. The two causes found: Farmer's
  minimap markers gathered, sorted and re-placed every saved spot once a
  second because the clock second was in their redraw key (now only the
  fade moves, `MapMarkers:FadeMinimap`); and on every craft Tradeskills
  re-read every recipe in the profession window and repriced the whole
  craft pane in one frame (now `Recipes:NothingNewMidCraft` skips the
  re-read mid-run and `CraftPanel:RefreshInSlices` spreads the pane over
  frames). Visuals and usability are never traded away for speed. This
  rule is in all four addons' notes; keep them the same.
- **The client is WoW: Forever, a retail engine** (`## Interface: 16001`,
  modern Lua), not 1.12. Instances hand addons secret values that raise when
  compared, concatenated or formatted, so read client values inside a pcall.
  Registering an event the client does not know raises too, which is why
  `ns.RegisterEvent` wraps it.
